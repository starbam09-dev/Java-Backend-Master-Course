# Prompt: Lesson Authoring

Use this prompt when drafting a JBMC lesson.

## Instruction

Write the lesson according to `MASTER_BOOK.md`.

Do not change the required lesson section order.

Use the exact structure from `templates/Lesson.template.md`.

## Required Direction

- Explain why the learner needs the concept.
- Connect the concept to Spring Boot.
- Connect the concept to ERP.
- Prefer business-domain examples.
- Do not introduce future concepts without a short forward reference.

## Output

Produce a Markdown lesson draft only.
